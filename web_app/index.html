The Silent Fall Crisis

A senior slips at home late at night. Without monitoring, family may not know until much later. A delay can make injuries harder to treat and prolong the emergency before help arrives.

Wearables Get Forgotten

Most elderly people stop wearing panic buttons and smartwatches within weeks. They're uncomfortable, need charging, and carry social stigma at home.

Our Solution: Invisible Protection

Radar sensors mounted on walls are invisible and require zero effort from your parents. They work through walls, in the dark, and never need charging.

Why We Built This

We started with a familiar worry: what if someone we love needs help and no one is nearby? Hertz & Haven grew from that question into a simple goal — help families stay connected to the people and places they care about, while respecting the dignity and independence of older adults.
